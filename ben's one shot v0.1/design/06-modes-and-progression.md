# 06 · Modes & Progression — 1-on-1, Arcade, Original, Adventure

Four ways to play. All share the same combat core; they differ in structure,
economy and payoffs.

---

## STORY PREMISE (all story content)

The world's floating castle — **Aerie Valerius** — has swallowed the Prism
Stones that keep the skylands afloat. Its master, **Dr. Valerius** (a mad
scientist whose experiments twist stone, metal and flesh), is draining the
stones' power to lift the castle into the stratosphere and rule the skies.
Fighters from every skyland are captured and forced into arena trials
("the Chaos Games") for the doctor's amusement. Beat the games, smash the
machines, and escape the castle. Tone: comedic, Saturday-morning anime —
the doctor is dramatic and ridiculous, never grim.

---

## MODE 1 · 1-ON-1 STORY ("Duel for the Sky")

2 fighters, head-to-head through a chapter ladder. Story mode for a single
player or two local players.

| Ch | Stage | Opponent | Twist |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mirage Dune Works | Rival (same-archetype mirror) | Tutorial: dodge-tap, combos, items |
| 2 | Glacier Rumble | Zephyr or Hanabi (agile) | Slippery surfaces tutorial |
| 3 | Abyssal Submarine Bay | Rok (ranged) | Sub dive events |
| 4 | Sky Galleon Break-Up | Gallus (brute) | Free-fall tutorial |
| 5 | Tomb of the Sun King | Camellia (stance duelist) | Sand flood, vault items |
| 6 | Crumbling Sky Castle | Grimjaw (tank) | Collapsing walls, ballista |
| 7 | Orbital Prism Station | Ember (zoner) | Low gravity, laser grids |
| 8 | Chaos Lab | **Dr. Valerius** (boss) | Multi-phase boss |

* HP 450 per side, 150 s timer, no items in chapters 1–2 (learn to fight),
  normal items from chapter 3.
* Losing a match = retry from the start of the chapter (skippable cutscenes).
* **Ending**: character-specific ending comic + a playable **Aldric** teaser.
  Clear with Rex to unlock Aldric's story epilogue.

**Design note:** 1-on-1 teaches the dodge-tap and the stone loop in a low-noise
environment. Item density and stage chaos ramp up chapter by chapter.

## MODE 2 · ARCADE ("Chaos Games League")

4 fighters per bout across a 5-round ladder plus 2 boss fights.

| Round | Fighters | Stage pool | Advancement |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4 (1 human + 3 CPU) | Desert / Iceberg | **Top 2 advance** |
| 2 | 4 (survivors + seeded CPUs) | Submarine Bay | Top 2 |
| 3 | 4 | Tomb / Castle | Top 2 + **Mid-Boss: Sphinx-9** |
| 4 | 4 | Sky Galleon / Space Station | Top 2 |
| 5 (final) | 4 | Chaos Lab | Winner faces **Dr. Valerius** |

* HP 400, 180 s per bout. On timeout: HP% then KOs then gold decide.
* Eliminated fighters drop their gold into a pot split by the winners.
* **Scoring:** KOs 300, stone thefts 150, item KOs 100, Perfect Dodges 50,
  stage-hazard KOs 400, remaining HP ×2, gold collected ×1, streak bonus.
* **Winning streaks** across bouts multiply gold (×1.0 → ×2.0 over 5 wins) and
  give the leader a visible crown glow (cosmetic but taunt-relevant).
* Boss fights scale HP with surviving fighters: 1 fighter = 60% boss HP,
  2 = 80%, 3–4 = 100%. Everyone fights the boss (co-op against boss, then the
  winners duel it out afterward if time permits).
* **Continue**: 3 continues; each continue adds +10% boss HP (penalty).

## MODE 3 · ORIGINAL ("Free Battle")

The sandbox. Everything configurable:

| Option | Values |
|---|---|
| Fighters | 2–4, each Human/CPU/Off, CPU difficulty 1–5 |
| Teams | Free-for-all, 2v2, 1v3 (boss-run style), 2v1v1 |
| Stage | Any unlocked (8 + boss arenas), "Random", "Rotation" (stage change each round) |
| Time | 60 / 90 / 150 / 180 / 300 s / No limit |
| Win rule | Last standing, Most KOs, Highest score, Coin rush (gold goal), Stone rush (first to 3 transforms) |
| Items | Off / Sparse / Normal / Mayhem (×2) / Registered only (custom 5) |
| Hazards | Off / Low / Normal / Extreme |
| Stones | 2 / 4 / 8 / 12 live |
| Handicap | per-player HP/damage sliders (50–150%) |
| Friendly fire | On / Off |
| Stage events | On / Off / Accelerated (2× rate) |

* **Rotation mode** is the party staple: winner picks the next stage, losers
  get +8% Rare item chance.
* **Registered Handy Items** (doc 05 §10) customize what drops.
* Results screen: KOs, damage dealt, stones collected, Perfect Dodges, gold,
  best streak.

## MODE 4 · ADVENTURE ("Castle Run")

Roguelite-lite collection mode for 1–4 players (co-op or FFA, run rules
depend on team setting). Persistent meta-economy across runs.

### 4.1 Run structure

| Floor | Content | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Arena fights vs enemy waves on random stages | Gold, items, cards |
| 3 | Escort/Gimmick floor (ride mine carts / defend the reactor) | Big gold |
| 4 | Treasure floor (Tomb vault, no foes, 60 s gold rush) | Gold + card |
| 5 | **Mid-Boss: Sphinx-9** | Guaranteed card + Prism Shard |
| 6–7 | Arena fights, harder modifiers (fog, low gravity, mayhem items) | Items |
| 8 | Shop floor (spend) & Furnace floor (mix) | Crafting |
| 9 | Gauntlet (3 back-to-back bouts, HP persists) | Rare item |
| 10 | **Final Boss: Dr. Valerius** | Prism Stone + ending + unlocks |

* Per-fight **Item Stock**: you carry hand + 2 pockets between floors; the
  rest goes to the shared **Bag** (expandable to 20 slots).
* **Gold** is shared per-run in co-op; per-player in FFA.
* Losing a floor costs 30% gold and continues the run ("crash landing").

### 4.2 Item Shop ("Bram's Bazaar")

Bram the Broker runs the shop. Prices scale with run progress (+10% per floor).

| Category | Examples | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Common items | Bomb, Sabre, Bread | 30–60 |
| Uncommon items | Machine Gun, Shield, Bear Trap | 90–160 |
| Rare items | Bazooka, Wings, Elixir | 220–360 |
| Cards | any of the 10 | 150–300 |
| Bag slot | +1 (max 20) | 250 |
| Handy slot | unlock 4th/5th registered slot | 1000 / 2000 |
| Revive | Continue for a KO'd player | 500 |
| Mystery Box | random Rare+ | 200 |

* Sell price = 40% of buy price. Sell junk (scrap!) to fund card purchases.
* **Shopkeeper unlock**: buy every catalog item at least once → Bram becomes
  playable (12th/13th roster reveal).

### 4.3 Furnace (crafting: 2 items + 1 card)

Place **two items + one card** into the furnace. Success yields a new (often
better) item; failure yields **Scrap** (sellable, 15 gold) or a joke item.

| Recipe (Item A + Item B + Card) | Result | Fail result |
|---|---|---|
| Sabre + Bomb + Flame Card | Flame Blade | Scrap |
| Broadsword + Iron Cape + Stone Card | Beam Saber | Scrap |
| Revolver + Machine Gun + Gale Card | Nailgun | Scrap |
| Bomb + Dynamite + Chaos Card | Time Bomb | Whoopee Cushion |
| Bread + Golden Apple + Feast Card | Chefs Special | Poison Mushroom |
| Roller Blades + Wings + Gale Card | Jetpack | Scrap |
| Shield + Mirror Shard* + Stone Card | Mirror Shield | Scrap |
| Spear + Boomerang + Gale Card | Scythe | Scrap |
| Any sword + Thunder Card + Chaos Card | Thunder Blade | Rubber Chicken |
| Any sword + Ice Card + Gale Card | Ice Blade | Snow Globe |
| Spoon + Magazine + Mirage Card | Rubber Chicken | Plunger |
| Mine + Bear Trap + Mirage Card | Adhesive Spray | Smoke Bomb |
| Bomb + Holy Water + Hero Card | Prism Shard | Scrap |
| Wings + Jetpack + Hero Card | Prism Stone | Overdrive Orb |
| 3× Scrap + Gold Card | 100 gold | Scrap |

\* Mirror Shard = found in Adventure treasure floors.

* **Success rate** = 55% base + 5% per "Crafting Badge" earned from quests +
  10% if the card matches an item's element. Every successful recipe is
  recorded in the **Mix Book**; completing categories rewards permanent
  bonuses (e.g., all Fire recipes → +5% fire damage).
* **Failed mixes produce Scrap** (or junk) — never nothing.

### 4.4 Collection & Meta-progression

| Collection | Completes when | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Item Registry | own every item once | Bram unlock |
| Card Deck | all 10 cards owned | Chaos Card recipe unlock |
| Mix Book | 15 recipes | +10% forge success |
| Diary Pages (3) | found in Tomb/Space/Lab floors | Aldric unlock |
| Boss Medals | S/A/B rank on both bosses | Titles & cosmetics |
| Gold Hoard | 20,000 total gold | Golden Statue item upgrade |
| Streaks | 5-win streak | Crown cosmetic + gold multiplier |

* **Register up to 5 Handy Items** here (doc 05 §10) to shape Free Play drops.
* Save data: `save/profile.json` — gold, unlocks, registry, mix book, stats.

## Modes → Player Expectations Summary

| Concern | How it's served |
|---|---|
| "I want 30-second chaos" | Original + Rotation + Mayhem items |
| "I want a story" | 1-on-1 Story + Arcade |
| "I want progression" | Adventure (gold, cards, shop, furnace, unlocks) |
| "I want mastery" | Arcade scoring, boss ranks, Perfect Dodge tech, furnace recipes |
| "I have 4 people and no patience" | Original FFA, Chaos Lab, Extreme hazards |
