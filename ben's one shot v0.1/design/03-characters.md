# 03 · Character Roster & Movesets

13 playable fighters. Every character has unique ground attacks, air attacks,
quirks, a powered Stone form, two Power Drives and two Power Fusions.

**Stat scale** is 0–10 (HP is shown as a 1–10 survivability rank; concrete HP
pools are in the tuning table at the end).

Format per fighter: **Stats → Normal moveset → Powered form (Power Drives →
Power Fusions) → Quirks & playstyle.**

## Moveset Authoring Rules (for content pipeline)

1. Every fighter must define exactly: 3 ground attacks, 1 air, 1 dash, 1
   throw, 1 Action gimmick, 1 powered melee variant, 2 Power Drives, 2 Power
   Fusions, 2 quirks. Missing data falls back to Rex's template.
2. Frame data lives in `data/characters/<id>.json` and is hot-reloadable.
3. Damage/HP values above are starting points; balance targets: average TTK
   (time to KO) = 22–35 s in a 1v1, 35–60 s in 4-player free-for-all.
