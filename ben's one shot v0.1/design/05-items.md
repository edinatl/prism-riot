# 05 · Items — The Complete List

Items are the vocabulary of chaos. ~75 items across 10 categories plus
Adventure cards and stage fixtures. Every run should see something new.

**Global rules**
* Hold **one item in hand** (visible, thrown away with **Discard**) plus up to
  **2 handy items in pockets** (used with Action when the hand is empty).
* **Action** picks up / uses items. **Attack** swings, fires or strikes with
  the held weapon. **Discard** throws the held item forward (throwing items is
  a legit attack).
* Item boxes: 3 hits to break (or 1 heavy hit / explosion). Drop table in §4.
* Thrown items deal their "Throw" damage and have revive-pickup of 12 s on the
  floor before despawning.

## 10. Handy Item Registration (up to 5)

In Adventure and Original modes, players may **register up to 5 "handy items"**
that then appear in Free Play item pools.

* Registration happens in the Item Shop / Collection menu.
* Registered items in Free Play get +35% drop weight (not guaranteed).
* Duplicates do not stack; a registered item never appears twice in one box.
* Slots unlock over time: 2 by default, +1 per Arcade clear, cap 5.
* The designers' "starter five": Bomb, Machine Gun, Giant Mallet, Wings,
  Golden Apple.

### Fixed spawns
* Each stage seeds 3–4 "rich" boxes with a guaranteed Uncommon+.
* Food is guaranteed in at least 2 locations on every stage.
* Every stage has 1 guaranteed weapon-class box near the center.

## 12. Full-Item Interaction Matrix (design rules)

1. Any held item can be thrown (Discard) and hurts on impact (its Throw dmg).
2. Explosives hurt the thrower at 50% (self-damage is funny, not lethal).
3. Fire items ignite Oil Slicks, Bomb racks and wooden props.
4. Ice items freeze water surfaces into walkable ice for 10 s.
5. Shields break after their absorb value; Mirror reflects only projectiles.
6. Healing items are usable at full HP (wasteful but allowed — "flex heal").
7. Items in pockets survive knockdowns; the item in hand flies out on hit.
8. Vehicles cannot be combined with held items; boarding stows the held item.
